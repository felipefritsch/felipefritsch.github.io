OXFORD · MSc DISSERTATION · 2026

Deep Learning Models of Loan Portfolios and Asset-Backed Securities

A loan’s value depends on the path its borrower takes through repayment, delinquency, default and prepayment. The dissertation compares models of those transitions, examines what borrower history adds, and connects predictive performance to portfolio cashflows and valuation.

Supervised by Prof. Justin Sirignano. Research findings and limitations are documented in the dissertation.
